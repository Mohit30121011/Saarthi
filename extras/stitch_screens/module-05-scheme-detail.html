Scheme Objective & Scope

The Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna was instituted by the Government of Maharashtra to eliminate financial barriers for meritorious students from Economically Backward Classes (EBC), Open, and Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC). The scheme reimburses approved tuition and exam fees directly to higher learning institutions or beneficiary bank accounts.

Coverage Highlights

Applicable across 1,400+ Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy, Management & Medical Institutes

Statutory Formula

Benefit disbursement is tiered based on institute classification (Government, Aided, Private Unaided, or Deemed University) recognized by DTE, DHE, and State CET Cell.

Step-by-Step Application Workflow

1
MahaDBT Profile & Aadhaar Authentication

Register on the state portal using your 12-digit Aadhaar number with biometric or OTP verification. (Pre-filled from SAARTHI dossier).

2
Select Department of Higher & Technical Education (HED)

Navigate to Post-Matric Scholarships > Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna.

3
Course Details & CAP Application ID Linking

Provide your State CET Application ID (EN23908) to fetch your allotted institution and verified course fee structure automatically.

4
Institutional Scrutiny & Joint Director (DTE) Endorsement

Your college scholarship nodal officer verifies attendance, marks, and bonafide credentials before passing the claim to DTE Divisional Joint Director.

5
Direct PFMS Electronic Fund Transfer (DBT)

First installment disbursed directly to college treasury; student maintenance allowances deposited into Aadhaar-linked NPCI bank account within 30 days of sanction.
