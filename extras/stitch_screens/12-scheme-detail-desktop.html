About the Scheme

The Post-Matric Scholarship for Other Backward Class (OBC) students is an affirmative welfare initiative administered by the Government of Maharashtra under the Social Justice & Special Assistance Department. The scheme aims to provide full financial support to meritorious and economically underprivileged students belonging to backward communities to pursue recognized post-matriculation or secondary/higher education courses (XI, XII, Diploma, Degree, Postgraduate, and Professional technical courses) in approved institutions across India.

Assistance includes complete reimbursement of tuition fees, examination fees, compulsory educational charges, and a monthly maintenance stipend directly deposited into the citizen's Aadhaar-linked bank account under the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) framework.

Key Entitlements & Benefits

100% Tuition & Exam Fee Waiver

Complete reimbursement of approved college tuition, library, laboratory, and semester examination charges.

Monthly Living Maintenance Allowance

Up to ₹1,200 per month (hostellers) or ₹600 per month (day scholars) for ten academic calendar months.

Study Tour & Thesis Grants

Additional annual allowance of up to ₹1,600 for postgraduate research scholars and field study projects.

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Direct Deposit

No intermediary delays; payments credited directly into Aadhaar NPCI-mapped bank savings accounts.

Step-by-Step Application Roadmap

Follow this 4-step verified workflow to complete your MahaDBT submission.

1
Step 1 · Aadhaar Authentication

Register on the MahaDBT Citizen Portal

Visit mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in and login using your Aadhaar OTP credentials. Verify that your bank account seeding status indicates "Active NPCI Mapping".

2
Step 2 · Profile Completion

Fill Personal, Domicile & Income Details

Upload verified PDF copies of your Domicile, Caste Certificate, and Tehsildar Income Certificate. Use the Saarthi Document Checklist to ensure zero discrepancies.

3
Step 3 · Scheme Selection

Navigate to "Social Justice & Special Assistance"

Select "Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students". Select your college department, course name, and current academic year from the drop-downs.

4
Step 4 · Scrutiny & College Approval

Submit Application & Track Application ID

Download the generated application PDF receipt. College nodal officers complete verification within 7 working days, followed by District Social Welfare sanction.
